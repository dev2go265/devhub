Chapter 1

01Chapter 1

It started in a Discord server.

Devs2Go began as a little side project in a Discord server: a place where new developers could come to get hired, and where people looking to get stuff made could come to get it from a reliable source.

It flopped.

02Chapter 2

The pivot.

So I started the website instead: a place for people to get custom websites made by me, and hopefully, down the road, a place where those developers can come to get work too.

03Chapter 3 · About me

Who runs it.

I started building websites in high school, just messing around because I liked figuring out how things worked. It wasn't supposed to turn into anything serious.

But once I got decent at it, people started asking me to build stuff for them, and Devs2Go grew out of that.

It's still just me.

Whoever you talk to is the same person who writes the code, figures out the design, and gets the site live.

04Chapter 4

Where it's headed.

Right now Devs2Go is just me, but the plan isn't to stay a one-person shop forever. I want to grow it into something bigger where other developers can come on and get hired through Devs2Go too.

I know what it's like to be young, self-taught, and looking for a real shot to prove yourself. If this grows the way I hope, I want it to give other people like me that same chance.

Ring end gap on a real Briggs and Stratton

What you are looking at

  • The piston ring is pushed down into the cylinder bore, square, with the piston upside down.
  • A feeler gauge blade is slid into the gap between the ring ends.
  • The blade that slides in with a light drag is the end gap.
  • 130G32 top ring: standard 0.010 in, reject 0.040 in. Try it in the lab.

Camshaft and tappets

Watch the lobes

Each egg-shaped lobe pushes its tappet (lifter) up once per turn. In your 130G32 the tappet then pushes a push rod, which tips a rocker arm, which opens the valve. Tappet part number: 591449. Push rod: 590658.

Valves, springs, and seats (cutaway)

Find these

  • Valve heads sealing on their angled seats (45 degrees on your engine).
  • Valve springs pulling each valve shut.
  • Valve stems sliding in their guides.

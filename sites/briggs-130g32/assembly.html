    Why this order?
    • Safety first: the spark plug wire comes off first and goes back on last, so the engine cannot fire while your hands are in it.
    • Outside to inside: you have to remove what covers a part before you can reach it. The blower housing hides the flywheel. The head hides the valves. The cover hides the cam and crank.
    • Reassembly is the reverse, with new gaskets, clean parts, oil on moving surfaces, and every bolt tightened to spec. Spark plug: 180 lb-in. Head bolts, rod bolts, flywheel nut, and cover: check the Briggs repair manual.
    • Label and photograph the governor linkage and spring holes before you take them off.

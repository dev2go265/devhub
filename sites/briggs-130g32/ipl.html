"Optional" parts are ones the 130000 operator's manual marks "if installed" (electric start, alternator, key switch, low-oil sensor, gear reduction, flat-panel air cleaner, high-altitude jet), so your engine may not have them. "Alternate" means the IPL lists more than one version of that part and only one fits your engine: match it to the part on your engine.

The big six dangers

⛽ Fuel

Gasoline vapor explodes. Fill the tank outdoors with the engine off and cool. No sparks, flames, or smoking. Wipe up spills right away. Store fuel in an approved can.

♨ Hot muffler

The muffler and cylinder stay hot long after shutdown and can burn skin or start a fire. Let the engine cool before you touch it or refuel. Keep grass and rags away from it.

⚙ Spinning flywheel

The flywheel and anything on the PTO shaft can grab fingers, hair, sleeves, and strings. Never reach in while it is running. Keep the blower housing on.

👓 Eyes

Wear safety glasses for all shop work: springs, compressed air, cleaners, and fuel can all hit your eyes.

⚡ Disconnect the spark plug

Pull the spark plug wire and ground it before you work on the engine or anything it drives. Turning the blade or shaft by hand can start it.

💨 Exhaust

Exhaust has carbon monoxide: no smell, no color, and it can kill. Only run engines outside or with a shop exhaust system.

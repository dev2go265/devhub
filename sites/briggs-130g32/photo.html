Tips for a good engine photo
  • Take it straight on, in good light, with the whole engine in the frame.
  • Take one from each end: the PTO end and the starter end.
  • For the official diagram: open the IPL for 130G32-0022-F1 on briggsandstratton.com, screenshot the exploded view, and upload it here.
  • In Quiz mode the labels turn into numbers. The site names a part, and you tap the right number.

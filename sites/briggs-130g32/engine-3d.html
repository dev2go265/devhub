What is in this model, and what is not
  • In the model: crankcase and cylinder, cylinder head, rocker cover, head gasket, crankcase cover, crankshaft and its output (PTO) end, crankshaft gears, governor gear and cup, the flywheel with its cooling fan, starter cup, recoil starter, blower housing, carburetor and float bowl, governor arm and linkage, fuel tank and cap, fuel line, air cleaner, muffler guard, spark plug, and about 300 bolts, nuts, and washers.
  • Moved in from a second CAD file: the piston, piston pin, connecting rod, camshaft, two tappets, two valves, and two valve springs came from a different engine model ("engine assembly", 2012). They were resized to fit this engine's 6.75 cm bore and lined up on its crankshaft and cylinder, so they show where each part goes. Their exact shape and size are not this engine's.
  • Still missing: the ignition coil, separate piston rings, and the push rods and rocker arms (the second file's push rods are too long for this engine). Learn those with the 4-stroke simulator and the part cards.
  • About 320 small parts (bolts, nuts, washers, clips) were matched to the part they sit next to, so they come off with that part. Parts we could not identify for sure are called "Bracket" or "Cover" instead of guessing.
  • Model: the triangle mesh stored inside the "XR550" SolidWorks file, split into 394 parts and simplified to 25% of its triangles for the web.

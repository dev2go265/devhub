Stuck? How each lab tool works
  • Micrometer tasks: read the close-up: sleeve first, then the thimble, then add. 1-2 in and 2-3 in micrometers add 1.000 or 2.000.
  • Hole tasks (bores, bearings, guides): a telescoping or small hole gauge picks up the size, then you read it with a micrometer. The close-up shows that micrometer.
  • Feeler gauge tasks (ring gaps, side clearance): try blades. Loose = too thin. Will not go in = too thick. Light drag = your reading.
  • Cylinder bore: measure across the bore two ways, 90 degrees apart. Out-of-round = big reading minus small reading. Reject is 0.0015 in or more.
  • Use the spec table if you need the numbers.

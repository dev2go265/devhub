Stuck? How to find each spot
  • Top: the fuel tank and cap sit up high so gravity feeds the carburetor.
  • Left (flywheel side): the blower housing covers the flywheel and the Magnetron coil. The recoil starter is on the outside of it.
  • Middle: the cylinder with its cooling fins points up. The cylinder head is on top, with the spark plug in it.
  • Right: the carburetor and air cleaner bring air in. The muffler lets exhaust out.
  • Bottom: the crankcase holds the oil. The drain plug is low. The PTO crankshaft comes out of the crankcase cover.

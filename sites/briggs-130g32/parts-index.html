How to use this index
  • Group headings are the official IPL (Illustrated Parts List) diagram groups for 130G32-0022-F1, revision 23-Nov-2020. Tap a heading to open that diagram.
  • "Not listed separately" means the IPL has no number for that piece by itself. It comes as part of a bigger assembly, which is named.
  • Flywheel: the number depends on the build date. Use the model decoder to read your date code.
  • +.020 parts (piston 592956, rings 592958) are oversize. They are only for a cylinder that has been bored 0.020 in bigger. They are not sold on the Briggs store.

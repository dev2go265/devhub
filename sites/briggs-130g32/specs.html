    How Reuse or Replace works
    • Standard is the size of a brand-new part.
    • Reject is the worn-out size. At or past this number, the part is done.
    • Past reject: Replace. Example: an intake valve stem at 0.207 in is smaller than the 0.208 in reject. Replace the valve.
    • Within standard: Reuse. Example: a piston pin at 0.708 in is right at standard. Reuse it.
    • Close to reject: Re-measure. Clean the part, let it cool, re-zero your micrometer, and measure again in 2 or 3 spots. Then decide. On this site "close" means the last 25% of the space between standard and reject (at least 0.0005 in).
    • Which way does wear go? Shafts, pins, and stems wear smaller (reject "or less"). Holes, bores, bearings, and gaps wear bigger (reject "or more").
    • Out-of-round: measure the bore in two directions, 90 degrees apart. Subtract the small reading from the big one. 0.0015 in or more is reject.

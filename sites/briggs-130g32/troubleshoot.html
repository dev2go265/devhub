How pros troubleshoot
  • An engine needs 3 things: fuel, spark, and compression (plus air). Find which one is missing.
  • Easy and cheap first: check fuel, the fuel cap, the plug, and the air filter before you take anything big apart.
  • Listen to the story: "It hit a rock" points to the flywheel key. "It sat all winter" points to stale fuel.
  • Smoke color: blue = oil burning, black = too much fuel, white = water or a quick burst of oil after tipping.

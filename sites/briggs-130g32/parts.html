Gaskets on the lab engine

Carburetor, intake, and exhaust gaskets

Gaskets seal two metal surfaces so air, fuel, and exhaust cannot leak out where parts bolt together. Always use new gaskets when you put the engine back together.

See the cards for the head gasket, crankcase gasket, muffler (exhaust) gasket, and intake manifold.

Part cards

How to read a part card
  • Big name is the technical name your instructor and the manual use. The line under it is the plain-English name.
  • Official name notes show when Briggs and Stratton calls it something different in the IPL (Illustrated Parts List).
  • Official part number comes from the IPL for 130G32-0022-F1. When it says "Check the official IPL", the number was not verified, so look it up on briggsandstratton.com using your full model, type, and code.
  • Memory trick is the hook that helps it stick.

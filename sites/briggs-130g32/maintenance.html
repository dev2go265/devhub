From the Maintenance Schedule in the Briggs and Stratton 130000 Operator's Manual (80144064 rev A). Do whichever comes first: the hours or the year. Service more often in dusty conditions.

How to do each one

Oil change

  1. Pull the spark plug wire. Change the oil while the engine is warm (not hot), so it drains well.
  2. Remove the drain plug and drain into a pan.
  3. Reinstall the plug. Add oil slowly, checking the dipstick, to FULL. Capacity is 18 to 20 oz. Do not overfill (that causes blue smoke).
  4. Oil: SAE 30 above 40 °F (below that it starts hard). 10W-30, 5W-30, synthetic 5W-30, or Vanguard 15W-50 by temperature. Service class SF or higher.
  5. Recycle the old oil.

Air filter and pre-cleaner

  1. Loosen the fasteners and remove the cover.
  2. Carefully remove the pre-cleaner and the paper filter (590601) from the base.
  3. Tap the paper filter lightly on a hard surface to loosen dirt. If it is still dirty, replace it.
  4. Wash the foam pre-cleaner (590602) in liquid detergent and water. Let it air dry completely. Do not oil the pre-cleaner.
  5. Put the dry pre-cleaner on the filter, install them, and reinstall the cover.

Spark plug

  1. Pull the wire. Remove the plug with a spark plug socket.
  2. Check the color: light tan is good, black and sooty is rich, wet and oily is burning oil.
  3. Check the gap with a wire gauge: .030 in. Replace yearly with resistor plug 691043.
  4. Thread it in by hand first, then torque to 180 lb-in (20 Nm).

Cooling system

  1. Engine off and cool, plug wire off.
  2. Every use: clean the air intake grille and the area around the muffler and controls.
  3. Yearly (more often when dusty): remove the blower housing and clean grass and dirt out of the cooling fins.

Fuel and storage

  1. Use clean, fresh unleaded gas, 87 octane or higher, with no more than 10% ethanol. Never E15 or E85.
  2. Fuel can go stale in a can after 30 days. Add alcohol-free fuel stabilizer when you fill the can.
  3. For storage: if the fuel was not stabilized, drain it into an approved container and run the engine until it is out of fuel.
  4. Change the oil while the engine is still warm. Store it level, dry, and away from flames and pilot lights.

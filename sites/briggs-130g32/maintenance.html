Intervals follow the Briggs and Stratton operator's manual maintenance chart. Confirm them in the manual for your engine. Oil type: check the manual. Oil capacity: 20 oz.

How to do each one

Oil change

  1. Pull the spark plug wire. Run the engine a minute first if cold, so the oil drains well, then let it cool enough to handle.
  2. Remove the drain plug (or tip as the manual says) and drain into a pan.
  3. Reinstall the plug. Add oil slowly, checking the dipstick, to FULL. Capacity 20 oz. Do not overfill (that causes blue smoke).
  4. Recycle the old oil.

Air filter

  1. Remove the cover. Take out the foam pre-cleaner and paper filter (590601).
  2. Wash the foam in soapy water, dry it fully.
  3. Tap the paper filter gently to knock dirt off. Never wash, oil, or blow compressed air through paper. Replace it if dirty or damaged.

Spark plug

  1. Pull the wire. Remove with a spark plug socket.
  2. Check the color: light tan is good, black sooty is rich, wet oily is burning oil.
  3. Replace with resistor plug 691043. Gap: check the manual. Thread in by hand first.

Cooling fins

  1. Engine off and cool, plug wire off.
  2. Brush grass and dirt from the blower housing screen and around the cylinder.
  3. For packed fins, remove the blower housing and clean them.

Fuel storage

  1. Fresh fuel only: gas older than about 30 days starts to go stale and leave varnish.
  2. Add fuel stabilizer to the can, and run the engine a few minutes to get it into the carb. Or run the engine out of fuel.
  3. Store the engine level, dry, and away from flames.
